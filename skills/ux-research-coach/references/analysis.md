# Analysis: from interview text to insights and focus

Serves mode 2 (Разбор интервью), mode 5 (Фокусировка), mode 9 (Идеи) and mode 10 (Отчёт для руководства). Course content unless marked «вывод, не из курса».

## 1. Where this sits in the process

The process is a «двойной алмаз»: ДИВЕРГЕНЦИЯ → КОНВЕРГЕНЦИЯ → ДИВЕРГЕНЦИЯ → КОНВЕРГЕНЦИЯ.

| Phase | Stages | Thinking |
|---|---|---|
| Вдохновение | 1. Погружение | divergence: widen the picture |
| Решение | 2. Фокусировка; 3. Генерация идей | convergence to the key problem, then divergence (as many ideas as possible) |
| Внедрение | 4. Проверка идей, обратная связь; 5. Внедрение | convergence: ideas are tested and selected |

Analysing interviews is the start of **Фокусировка**. Extra interviews belong to «Погружение»; voting on ideas belongs
to «Генерация идей». Uncertainty is normal at this point («клубок» that untangles gradually); it is not removed by
another round of research or a detailed plan. After a test a return to any stage is allowed; if the problem was
chosen inaccurately, go back to Фокусировка.

## 2. Выгрузка знаний

Put on stickers **everything** heard, learnt, seen and found inspiring — this is critical for a quality solution.

- Avoid interpretations and judgements of the user's actions: write what was said and done.
- Ideas that came up during immersion go into a **separate cluster** (they are not findings).
- Mark **emotionally charged words and epithets**, positive («Круто! Кайф! Радует! Важно!») and negative («Плохо!
  Раздражает! Бесит! Подвёл! Невозможно!») — **especially negative**.

## 3. Reading one interview

1. **Steps of the scenario.** Lay the material along the client's path (touchpoints in time order, including before
   and after the service itself). For each point: действия, эмоции, потребности, люди, контекст, продукты и сервисы.
2. **Говорит — делает — чувствует (айсберг).** Put the respondent's words next to what he describes doing and what he
   feels. A mismatch («в целом всё нормально» next to three calls and anger) is the most valuable place: dig deeper,
   do not take the summary phrase as the finding.
3. **What to look for at the points:** emotions or indifference; repetitions, loops, too long waiting; unresolved
   questions, missing functionality; confusion and the company's opaque actions; pain points. Workarounds the client
   built (screenshots, notes, a personal contact) show a barrier he has learnt to overcome.
4. **Quotes.** Keep the client's own words, verbatim, with the step they belong to.
5. **Several transcripts — coding** (вывод, не из курса; a standard qualitative-analysis technique that structures the
   выгрузка знаний): **open coding** — mark the key fragments and give each a short code (theme), keeping verbatim
   quotes; **axial coding** — group the codes into categories and look for links and cause-and-effect chains between
   them; **selective coding** — find the central theme of the scenario and state the insights. Keep for every
   category which respondents it comes from.
6. **Status of conclusions.** Interviews give insights and **problem hypotheses** («гипотезы проблем») about the
   scenario; two respondents of one audience already let you draft a typical scenario; the project's norm is at least
   five interviews. The problem hypotheses are confirmed or refuted by a survey — without it a problem may turn out
   to be a fantasy or one person's problem.

## 4. Insight

**Инсайт — это открытие: почему что-то происходит, что является первопричиной** (also called «вывод»). It is not a
hypothesis and not a pain point. A pain point says *what* hurts («клиент долго ждёт»); an insight says *why the
behaviour or the feeling is the way it is* — the reason the client acts so.

A good insight is:
- **Информативный** — sheds light on what people really want;
- **Вдохновляющий** — motivates the team to act («готовы бежать и что-то делать»);
- **Запоминающийся** — short, capacious, easy to share.

A useful written form (вывод, не из курса): «[Кто] [что делает / чувствует], потому что [причина, найденная в
интервью] — [что из этого следует для сценария]». When the insight is about an unmet need, another form works:
«[Кто] нуждается в [потребность], потому что [причина], но сейчас [барьер]» (вывод, не из курса). Each insight points
to the quotes and steps it rests on.

The course contrast: an insight is not a hypothesis and not a pain point. Also not an insight (вывод, не из курса): a
solution («нужно push-уведомление»), a guess about the company's internals, a remark on research method, a statistic.

## 5. Persona model

Key characteristics and needs of the client archetype: возраст, род занятий, семейное положение; сегмент / архетип
в контексте задачи; цели и задачи в рамках сценария; потребности и проблемы; впечатление от исследуемого сервиса;
other criteria that may affect the scenario. Useful additions (вывод, не из курса): how often the persona uses the
service and one key quote in her own words. The course calls this whole list «атрибутивы персоны»; in the narrow
sense атрибутивы are the traits that set the persona apart and whose change changes the scenario (`interviews.md` §2).

## 6. Reports

**Interview analysis** (for the team): the course gives the parts, not a template; the skill's order (вывод, не из
курса): цель и сценарий → кто опрошен → путь по шагам с цитатами и эмоциями → расхождения «говорит / делает» →
инсайты with evidence → open questions and problem hypotheses → what to check next.

**For management — the investment-committee structure** (10 minutes of talk, 10 of feedback):
1. **Суть проекта:** какой процесс выбрали; какой контекст ситуации; какую проблему мы решаем.
2. **Целевая аудитория:** кто клиент; какая боль / проблема клиента; как звучит вопрос для поиска решений.
3. **Подтверждение проблемы:** инсайты из качественных исследований (2–3); какие гипотезы проблемы проверяли в
   количественных исследованиях; какую проблему выбрали в качестве ключевой.
4. **Варианты решения:** не менее 5 альтернатив; criteria (more than five, varied); each option removes the problem.

## 7. Focus: from insights to the project's question

Steps of Фокусировка:
1. On the basis of the research, determine the client's **key problems** in the scenario (which insights?).
2. Determine the **main problem** — the one that influences the result most and is worked on first. Not the most
   frequent complaint and not the easiest to remove. The survey of the problem hypotheses (`metrics.md` §2) shows
   which problems are confirmed, how significant and how frequent; the key problem is chosen after it.
3. Formulate the **question for searching solutions**, built on the client's problem.

Фокусировка fixes the direction of the search; it is not a test of solution hypotheses and not more immersion. After
the research the focus on the client grows stronger — the team does not drift back to business questions.

**Project task.** It answers: why something needs improving; which business problem we solve; the impressions of
which user scenario we improve. **The problem always includes the user**: who meets the difficulty and what happens
because of it.
- Right: «Клиентские менеджеры упускают контракты на лизинг из-за слишком долгого согласования договоров».
- Wrong: «Нужно автоматизировать…» (a solution); «Банк теряет позиции…» (no person).
Project card fields: какую проблему решаем; вопрос проектирования «Как мы можем улучшить сервисный сценарий для нашего
пользователя?» (which scenario exactly); possible solutions that come to mind at once — think wide, one or two
hypotheses are fine, but leave room to be surprised; context, search area and constraints (geographic, physical, age,
technological); for whom the solution matters, stakeholders on the company side; how we will know the problem is
solved, which digital metrics measure the result.

**Вопрос «Как мы можем…?»** focuses the design task on all the research data; each insight is turned into a «Как мы
можем» question. It rests on the client's problem; an answer to it produces ideas for removing exactly that problem.

| Wording | Verdict |
|---|---|
| «Как помочь клиенту разобраться в особенностях сервиса?» | right: from the client's problem |
| «Как повысить доходность на 25%?» | from the bank's metric: drift to business |
| «Автоматизировать проверку документов» | a ready solution, not a question |

Course example: «Как мы можем помочь КМ ускорить процесс согласования договоров?». An extended form (вывод, не из
курса) keeps the same base: «Как мы можем [действие] для [пользователь], чтобы [результат для него], учитывая
[ограничение]?». Too general a question fits any
project. Solving the client's problem solves the business problem too.

**Roles around a project.**

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Клиент проекта | whose scenario was chosen and whose experience is improved; described by a persona; a whole unit is never the client |
| Пользователь | goes through the scenario; employees are users too |
| Стейкхолдер | everyone involved in the scenario on the client's and the company's side; found and involved; a client manager whose behaviour is changed is a stakeholder |
| Владелец процесса | responsible for the process («кто и за что отвечает» is found during immersion); an executor is not the owner |

**Карта стейкхолдеров** — one of the Фокусировка methods: everyone involved in the scenario. A practical grouping
(вывод, не из курса): on the client's side; the company's front line; the company's back office; external
participants (partners, regulators, contractors). For each — role in the scenario and what changes for them.

## 8. Five key errors of a project

| Error | Sign |
|---|---|
| Слишком рано к решению | a ready answer at once (automation, training, informing), the scenario is not assembled |
| Слабые интервью | 10–15-minute talks or questionnaires instead of in-depth interviews |
| Размытый сценарий | several similar paths are mixed; CJM, SB and the project card describe different processes |
| Низкая детализация CJM и SB | not synchronised; no split between the client's actions, the visible part and hidden processes |
| Слабая связка боли и метрики | a metric (conversion, churn) is declared, but the CJM does not show where it fails |

## 9. Ideas on the «Как мы можем» question (mode 9)

- **Мозгоштурм** — as many answers as possible to the key question. Seven rules: держим фокус; не критикуем; не
  перебиваем друг друга; развиваем диковатые идеи; картинка лучше слов; принцип «да и…» к любой идее; количество идей
  важнее. «Нереально, бюджета не будет» breaks «не критикуем».
- **Антиштурм** — ideas for the reversed question «Как мы можем ухудшить ситуацию для пользователя?», clustered by
  scenario point (these are search directions), then reversed and developed.
- **Переворот стереотипов** — write the stereotypes of the scenario, then «А что, если…?» (переворот, замена, отказ,
  мечта).
- **Отбор:** «Топ идеи» (each in silence picks the five most inspiring), «5 шляп» (генератор идей, оптимист,
  пессимист, техническая осуществимость, адвокат пользователя), criteria with weights (потребность, преимущества,
  осуществимость, затраты, риски); a good solution is востребовано, соответствует бюджету и осуществимо.
- Then a prototype and a test with clients (mode 8).
