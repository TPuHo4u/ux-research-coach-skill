# Journey: service scenario, CJM, Service Blueprint

Serves mode 3 (CJM) and mode 4 (Service Blueprint). Course content unless marked «вывод, не из курса».

## 1. Service scenario and the two formulas

**Сервисный сценарий** — the client's path through all touchpoints of interaction with a service (course example: a
trip from dreaming of a holiday to the review after returning). It is formulated **from the client's position**, to
find the critical points where the impression fails. It is not a front-line employee's regulation and not the
stages of a project.

**Впечатление клиента = Факт − Ожидание.** The rating is set not by the fact alone but by how it meets the
expectation.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Same service, different expectations | better for the one whose expectation is not above the fact; high expectations do not improve the impression |
| The fact cannot change (a long wait) | name the real time honestly and in advance: the expectation moves to the fact |
| Advertising «fast answer» with the same fact | raises the expectation, the impression gets worse |
| The procedure is set by law | make the reasons for waiting transparent, improve other touchpoints |

**Безупречный сервис = Система + Человек.** Система — regulations and information systems; Человек — the employee
and his attitude to the client. Regulations and IT work but the employee is formal → «Человек» is missing.

Theatre analogy: **сцена** = experience and impressions (what the client gets); **закулисье** = the process that
creates the impression. Touchpoints are where the client meets the company, not the backstage.

## 2. Customer Journey Map

**Путь клиента / CJM** — a single map of the client's interaction with the company, with defined touchpoints, the
client's actions and the products used, with all the emotions and barriers he meets while using the service. Not just
«the process of using a product» and not «steps from the application to getting the service».

**Three periods, in this order:**

| Period | What forms |
|---|---|
| Досервисный (pre-service) | Ожидание |
| Сервисный (service) | Фактический опыт |
| Пост-сервисный (post-service) | Впечатление, удовлетворённость |

**Touchpoint** — any contact of the client with the company along the whole scenario. The channel does not matter
(branch, call, app, site, letter, SMS); steps before and after the service itself count; interactions outside the
scenario do not. Counting only live contact with staff is a trap.

**What goes into a CJM:** the persona going through the scenario; a timeline; all touchpoints of client and company;
the client's actions and tasks at each point; channels; emotions along the whole route. Technical means and
employees' regulations are not CJM — they are Service Blueprint.

**Template rows** (one column per touchpoint, grouped by stages): этапы → точки контакта → описание точки контакта →
канал взаимодействия с сервисом → что клиент делает → задача клиента в этой точке → эмоциональные впечатления клиента
(a curve). Header: имя (persona), проект, дата. Understanding which task the client tries to solve at each point is a
huge potential for changing the scenario. A «Process ownership» row («кто и за что отвечает») links to the blueprint.

**Emotions** are there to find the **critical failure points**: points with negative emotions are marked as improvement
zones. Emotions are **not averaged** — an average hides exactly those points — and the team does not run to ideas:
first find the cause of the failure. Course example: an application for contract approval goes to the archive after
15 days and is filed again (four times) — emotions at the bottom.

**One scenario.** Several similar but different paths (site, app, branch) mixed in one map make it «размытый»: CJM,
blueprint and project card then describe different processes. Choose one path for the map, or show the branches
explicitly and say which one is studied (вывод, не из курса: a separate lane per channel).

**The template is not a holy grail.** It is support, not dogma. Keep the map on stickers: easy to change, remove,
rewrite. A beautiful map is a pity to «spoil», so inaccuracies stay; new interview data are a reason to update it.

**Facts and assumptions** (вывод, не из курса): mark each cell as seen in interviews or assumed; a CJM built «по
мнению экспертов» without research is a trap — immersion starts from the scenario, the audience and the research plan.

**Steps of immersion:** choose the scenario and the client → identify all interaction channels → plan and run the
research → describe all touchpoints with the client's eyes → find who is responsible for what inside the company,
build the process map (SB) → formulate the problems and design questions.

## 3. Service Blueprint

**Service Blueprint (общая схема сервиса)** unfolds the service scenario «вовнутрь компании»: what in processes,
actions, technical equipment or regulations creates the impression at each touchpoint. The service at a point is the
result of several teams' work.

**Layers from top to bottom:**
1. **Путь клиента** — the client's path through the scenario and his impressions (pre-service / service / post-service).
2. **Фронт-лайн** — who interacts with the client at each point and what actions they take.
3. **Линия видимости для клиента.**
4. **Back-office** — who or what creates these front-line actions: подразделения, регламенты, технические средства,
   действия сотрудников (several rows).

| Where | What is described |
|---|---|
| Above the line of visibility | status texts on the screen, the employee's answers on the line — the client's experience and the front line |
| Behind the line of visibility | the queue of applications by the regulation, the systems used, other units' actions |

The «общая схема сервиса» includes: the IT tools of the point; the processes that produce the result; instructions,
scripts, policies; processes that affect the employee (training, onboarding).

**Steps of building:**
1. **Interviews (or observation) with internal experts** from the units that affect the touchpoint: understand the
   processes, procedures, specialists' actions. The result is an understanding of all processes behind the line of
   visibility. Not a scheme drawn from instructions and not a survey of clients.
2. **Describe** the processes, procedures, actions of other units' employees and the technical means that affect the
   touchpoint but are invisible to the client. **One horizontal row = one process or set of actions; vertically it
   stands under the touchpoint the client named.**

**Order:** first the CJM and its failure point, then the blueprint. Other tools do not replace it: the persona
describes the client, the knowledge dump records what was heard and seen without interpretation.

**Traps:**
- building the blueprint «по инструкциям» instead of interviewing internal experts; a regulation's deadline is a
  claim to compare with the real practice, not the description of the process;
- grouping processes by department without tying them to touchpoints;
- low detail: CJM and blueprint not synchronised by steps, or done formally; no split between the client's actions,
  the visible part and hidden processes — the failure point cannot be found;
- a weak link between the pain and the business metric: the metric is declared but the map does not show where it
  fails.

Places where a delay or a failure usually hides (вывод, не из курса, as hypotheses to check in the expert interviews):
hand-offs between units, queues, a status that is not updated or not shown to the client, documents requested again,
no owner for a step.
