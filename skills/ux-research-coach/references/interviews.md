# Interviews: plan, guide, conduct

Serves mode 1 (Интервью) and the prototype test in mode 8. Course content unless marked «вывод, не из курса».

## 1. Why interviews, and what they can and cannot give

Qualitative research (интервью, наблюдение, «мокасины») immerses the team in the client's experience and finds pain
and inconvenience along the whole path. Its tasks: восстановить процесс «жизни клиента», найти точки провалов,
сформулировать гипотезы проблем. Already **two respondents of one audience** let you draft a typical scenario. The
course's project asks for **at least two research methods**: interviews with **at least five clients** and a second
method of the team's choice used **at least three times**. How widespread a problem is, interviews do not show — that
is the job of a survey (`metrics.md`). The cause of a dropped index is found the other way round: by in-depth
interviews and observation.

A 10–15-minute chat or a questionnaire is not an in-depth interview («слабые интервью» is one of the five key
errors of a project).

## 2. Research plan (before any question)

| Part | What to fix |
|---|---|
| Область погружения | one service scenario, said from the client's side, and its blocks (stages) to light up |
| Кто | respondents who fit the persona on every attribute: users (main **and** extreme), internal and external experts, visible and invisible participants of the scenario |
| Методы | which of: глубинное интервью, наблюдение, мокасины, поиск аналогов, интервью с экспертами, экстремальные пользователи, кабинетные исследования |
| Роли и время | who recruits, who collects data, who observes, who leads the interview; plan the time |

**Methods, when to use them.**

| Method | What it gives |
|---|---|
| Глубинное интервью | the best way to understand hopes, expectations, impressions and the client's vision |
| Наблюдение | non-verbal signals and inconveniences the client no longer notices (he is used to overcoming the barrier) |
| Мокасины | the team walks the client's path itself, sees the service with his eyes, feels what he feels |
| Поиск аналогов | a fresh view: in which industry is a similar problem already solved? |
| Интервью с экспертами | quickly name the key problem areas, share insights, stories, context; usually stays at their general vision |
| Экстремальные пользователи | the most unusual view; a solution for everyone works only if it works for them |

**Persona and attributes.** Research focuses on one characteristic person (an archetype), not «всех клиентов»: too
wide an audience hides the weak spots. Balance: too narrow — respondents are hard to find; too wide — the data are
too general. **Атрибутивы** are the traits that set the persona apart and change her decisions, actions, reactions,
emotions: change an attribute and the scenario changes. Good: «мама с ребёнком, с плотным расписанием внеклассных
занятий»; bad: «все сотрудники Компании».

**Recruiting.** Yourself or with help; state precisely whom to look for; every respondent matches the persona model.

**Internal client.** A scenario of an internal client is researched by interviewing both the clients and the
internal experts of the neighbouring units. Employees are users of the service too: there are always two sides of
the interaction, and the employee's path is worth mapping as well.

Not from the course, usually needed in practice (mark «вывод, не из курса» when used): consent to record and how the
recording is stored; a neutral invitation that does not reveal the hypothesis; a small thank-you if the company
allows it; 45–60 minutes per interview.

## 3. Interview dynamic (U-curve)

1. **Знакомство, разогрев** — general, simple, unobtrusive questions; let the person relax; the general impression of
   the scenario and its stages.
2. **Фокусировка** — narrow to the scenario under study.
3. **История** — the deepest point: the last real case, or «самый неприятный или долгий случай», step by step, at each
   point of the scenario.
4. **Проблематика идеи** — only after the story: problems, wishes, reactions to an idea.
5. **Завершение, расслабление** — wrap up, thank, ask what was not asked.

Focus comes before the story, the idea part after it; a guide that opens with «что улучшить?» skips the deepest part.

## 4. Guide: blocks and question rules

The guide has two blocks: **«Общие вопросы»** (start the talk, win the person over, reveal the general scenario and
its stages) and **«Идите глубже»** (hopes, fears, ambitions and expectations at each stage; what he saw, how he acted,
why, and what impression it left). Prepare the questions and know why each is asked; plan questions about
motivation; look for the person's vision of the scenario, not separate facts; give a chance to draw the scenario.

**Опыт, а не мнение.**
- Ask about experience, not opinion; about the present and the past, not the future.
- Avoid «часто / редко / иногда» and scales 0–10: everyone means a different number.
- Meet in the person's usual context; ask him to show how he usually goes through the scenario.

Course examples.

| Errors | Right |
|---|---|
| «Процесс … для вас неудобен?» | «Как вы решаете сейчас эту задачу / проблему?» |
| «Есть ли у вас какие-нибудь проблемы?» | «Расскажите о вашем последнем случае, как это было?» |
| «Что вам кажется нужно улучшить?» | «Почему (делаете, действуете) таким образом?» |
| «А если бы …, вам было бы удобно?» | «А что именно происходило в этой ситуации?» |

The first two errors presuppose a difficulty; the last two ask an opinion and the future. The course's guide examples
also use «Покажите, как вы оформляете…», «Вспомните последний…», «Могли бы вы рассказать, как это происходило
поэтапно?», «Расскажите самый неприятный или долгий случай… Что было причиной? Как вы решили эту ситуацию?» and a
frequency question («Как часто?») — the ban is on «часто / редко / иногда» as answer gradations, not on asking how
often. A follow-up «Что было дальше? Что вы сделали потом?» keeps the story going (вывод, не из курса).

**«Лестница вопросов».** Time (прошлое, настоящее, будущее) × эмоции, факты, ценности; a good question sits at the
present, between facts and values.

**Question types** (useful to recognise; open questions carry the interview):

| Type | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Открытые | a full, detailed answer | «Что для вас самое важное в …?» |
| Закрытые | to clarify or check understanding | «Правильно ли я понимаю, что …?» |
| Альтернативные | to reach a conclusion between options | «Какая гипотеза правдоподобнее — А или Б?» |
| Чувственные | to confirm a decision already taken | «Вы же хотите найти наилучшее решение?» |

Questions that manipulate the answer occur, on average, in 40% of interview recordings. A «чувственный» question
pushes the answer, so it does not belong in a research interview (вывод, не из курса, from the course's warning about
manipulation).

**Techniques for depth:** «Нарисуй меня» (draw your path), «Покажи мне» (show how you do it), «Расскажи мне
историю», «5 Почему?» (ask why several times). The person answers in general phrases → ask him to draw the path or to
show it.

**Remember about people:** not everything can be said in words — people think in pictures; even the most rational
decisions contain emotions; a person may not be aware of the true motives; people are lazy to think through overly
complex questions. «Клиент всегда говорит правду. Он не всегда прав»: in his head it is so.

**Interviewer errors:** вопрос — ответ — следующий вопрос (no follow-up); «Понятно» in the middle of a phrase;
professional terms; lecturing the respondent. Coming with a ready idea, you see only its confirmation.

## 5. What to capture during the interview

**Objects of research** at every point: **действия** (что люди делают), **эмоции** (чувства и ощущения),
**потребности** (для чего им это), **люди** (кто вовлечён со стороны клиента и компании), **контекст** (место и
окружение), **продукты и сервисы** (what causes difficulty).

**Айсберг:** what people **говорят** is the visible part; what they **делают** and **чувствуют** is the main hidden
part. Compare words with behaviour and emotions; a mismatch is a reason to dig deeper.

**What to look for at the points of the scenario:** emotions, impressions or indifference; repetition and loops,
too long waiting; unresolved questions, missing functionality; the client's confusion and the company's opaque
actions; pain points.

Write down observations and the client's own quotes (verbatim), and emotionally charged words.

**Observation sheet** — one per critical point of the scenario (some inconveniences the client will not name because
he is used to overcoming them): точка сценария; какие действия происходят; кто участвует со стороны пользователя и
компании; какая информация используется, какой не хватает клиенту; какие продукты / сервисы используются; что
физически окружает клиента; что уже работает на позитивное впечатление; что работает неэффективно; дополнительные
замечания.

## 6. Interviews with internal experts (for a Service Blueprint)

Interview or observe the experts of every unit that affects the touchpoint: understand processes, procedures,
specialists' actions, systems used, hand-offs. The result is an understanding of everything behind the line of
visibility. A regulation or an instruction is what should happen; the interview finds what actually happens. Useful
questions (вывод, не из курса, built on the course's aim): «Покажите, что вы делаете, когда приходит заявка»; «От
кого вы её получаете и кому передаёте?»; «Что происходит, если документов не хватает?»; «Где вы видите статус?»;
«Когда в последний раз всё пошло не так — что было?».

## 7. Testing a prototype with clients

The aim of a test is to find the flaws and get the most objective feedback, not to show the solution in a good
light. Tell the client this is a first prototype; adjust it during the session to test ideas he voices. A storyboard
(a quick cheap visualisation of the scenario with the idea) or a rapid prototype (from materials at hand, testing the
critical artefacts of the scenario) checks the scenario — a survey or an expert opinion does not.

Structure the feedback, and discuss it with the team after each session:

- **Что сработало?** What did they like; what was most valuable; what did they react to positively?
- **Что не сработало?** What would you change now; were there suggestions; what did you learn that can improve it?
- **Что было вдохновляющим?** What happened that you did not expect; what surprised you; what to test next time;
  which new client needs did you learn?

The test results are one more piece of research: refine the prototype with the most valuable remarks; a return to
any earlier stage is normal («не бойтесь пересмотра идеи»). Then: минимально работающий прототип (test, feedback,
return to any stage) → MVP (market data before large spending) → пилот (a long test before the market).
