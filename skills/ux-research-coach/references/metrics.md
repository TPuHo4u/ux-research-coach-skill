# Metrics and surveys: checking hypotheses, NPS, CSI, CSAT

Serves mode 6 (Опрос) and mode 7 (Индексы). Course content unless marked «вывод, не из курса».

## 1. Qualitative vs quantitative

| | Qualitative | Quantitative |
|---|---|---|
| Methods | интервью с экспертами, экстремальные пользователи, наблюдение, поиск аналогов, мокасины, глубинные интервью | NPS, CSI, CSAT, обращения, ГВК, FCR, опросы различного формата |
| Tasks | восстановить процесс «жизни клиента»; найти точки провалов; сформулировать гипотезы проблем | проверить проблему (гипотезу) на всей целевой аудитории; подтвердить значимость; оценить периодичность |
| Volume | from 2 respondents of one audience a typical scenario can be drafted | relevant only on large volumes: not less than 5% of the audience |

Quantitative methods «снимают» the impression; why it dropped is found by in-depth interviews and observation.
A satisfaction survey and the indices are quantitative, not qualitative. ГВК — «голос внутреннего клиента», a survey of
internal clients on the CSI principle. FCR is only named in the course (вывод, не из курса: first contact
resolution — the share of requests solved at the first contact).

## 2. Survey that checks the hypotheses of the interviews

**Purpose.** The survey confirms or refutes the **problem hypotheses** that came out of qualitative research — on the
whole target audience: whether the problem is there, how significant it is for the client, how often it occurs.
Satisfaction and recommendation are not measured in it and 1–10 scales are not used; NPS and CSI are not run by the
team — the existing index reports are analysed instead. Without the survey a problem may be «фантазия» or one
person's problem; at the defence the team says what percentage of respondents confirmed the problem and its
importance.

**The hypothesis.** An assumption to confirm or refute: formulated clearly, «однозначно бинарной», and not as a
negation. Sources: conclusions of qualitative research, trends, metric measurements, market and competitor analysis.

**Structure.**

| Block | Task | Course examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Идентификация пользователя | a «hygienic» screen-out of the non-target audience | «Пользовались ли вы кредитной картой в последние 3 месяца?» (да / нет); «Как часто вы пользуетесь кредитной картой?» (1–3 раза в неделю; 1–3 раза в месяц; 1–3 раза в год); «Являетесь ли вы клиентом …?» |
| 2. Опыт в проблемной области | did it happen and how often (the block «может включаться») | «Сталкивались ли вы с ситуацией, когда …?»; «Как часто возникает?» (один раз; 2–3 раза; всегда при совершении операции Х); «Предпринимали ли какие-то действия?» (при «да» — «опишите какие») |
| 3. Гипотезы | confirm or refute each hypothesis | specific questions per hypothesis |

**Making a good questionnaire** (the course's survey-team advice):
- 5–7 questions is normal; the more questions, the fewer answers to the last one; do not put your own work on the
  client.
- Screening questions confirm the target respondent; branching avoids irrelevant questions («я же написал, что не
  пользуюсь»); let the respondent skip a question he cannot answer.
- Do not ask what the databases already hold (a client who received a card is not asked whether he received it).
- Text clear to a person not immersed in the company's processes; short questions, not compound; no double questions
  («удобна и понятна» are two parameters); neutral, not leading («Как вы относитесь к…» rather than «Вам нравится…?»).
- «Другое» with a field for single and multiple choice; «Не взаимодействовал / Затрудняюсь ответить» when an employee
  is rated; random order of options.
- Question types: single and multiple choice, scale, matrix, free answer, ranking («распределите параметры по
  значимости») — ranking shows significance. Several scales in one survey are of one type. Comment fields are never
  mandatory. A greeting with the purpose at the start, thanks at the end. Surveys are usually anonymous.
- Closed questions (yes / no or a choice of quantitative options) are easier to process than open ones on large
  volumes.
- Frequency as concrete options, not «часто / редко»: everyone has his own meaning. Options covering the whole range,
  including «ни разу», are the skill's addition (вывод, не из курса).
- Without block 1 the sample includes people the problem does not concern.

**Channels:** on the site, by e-mail, in a digital channel, a personal or phone interview with one list of questions
for everyone («как допрос» with marks, so that answers are comparable), SMS.

**Sample.** Relevant from at least **5% of the target audience**; for a small audience a larger share (for about 100
people about 20%).

**Reporting.** Concrete numbers, not general phrases: «80% подтвердили проблему». The base is the target respondents
(those who passed block 1); the 5% rule is about the sample size, not the base of the percentage. A conditional
hypothesis is read among the respondents for whom the condition held (вывод, не из курса). The threshold at which a
hypothesis counts as confirmed is not given in the course — state it as an assumption (вывод, не из курса).

**An index request inside a hypothesis survey** («добавь NPS, начальник просит»): by the course, not added — the
existing reports give the index. If the user insists, it is a separate last question with the standard wording,
reported apart and never used as evidence for or against the hypotheses (вывод, не из курса).

## 3. NPS

**NPS (Net Promoter Score)** — the index of consumer loyalty («индекс чистой поддержки»), scale from 1 to 10. One
question with the focus on the highest marks: «Какова вероятность Вашей рекомендации компании другу или коллеге?» and a
request to explain the main reason for the mark.

| Mark | Group | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 10, 9 | Промоутеры | ready to keep buying and to recommend to friends and family |
| 8, 7 | Нейтралы | generally satisfied but not active promoters, or undecided |
| 6–1 | Детракторы (критики) | dissatisfied, inclined to negative reviews |

**% NPS = % промоутеров − % детракторов.** The base is all answers; neutrals stay in the base but are not counted in
the difference; the value may be negative. The course uses a 1–10 scale; on the common 0–10 scale a 0 is a detractor
(вывод, не из курса).
Пример для расчёта (не из курса): 50 answers; 10 and 9 — 30, 8 and 7 — 12, 6–1 — 8. Promoters 60%, detractors 16%,
NPS = 60 − 16 = 44%.

Traps: promoters are only 10 and 9 — an 8 is a neutral; the share of promoters alone is not NPS; adding neutrals to
«довольные» is not NPS.

A final NPS that did not change is no reason for calm. Look separately at the positive influence of promoters (how
often they recommend), the negative influence of detractors (how often they leave negative reviews) and the influence
of social networks. Fewer promoters and fewer dissatisfied — «разбухла серединка»: neutrals can leave to either side.

## 4. CSI

**CSI (Customer Satisfaction Index)** — the index of customers' satisfaction, scale 1 to 100, developed in 1989 by the
Stockholm School of Economics. Measures how satisfied the client is with a product or service — with the interaction
with the company or brand. By IBM data, +1 point on the 100-point scale ≈ +5 million dollars of income. Benchmarking
against industry and regional competitors. Used for adjusting processes, re-engineering, finding bottlenecks.

| Side | What to remember |
|---|---|
| Plus | evaluates a wide range of factors comprehensively; builds a model of satisfaction |
| Minus: price | harder and more expensive than NPS — more questions in the questionnaire |
| Minus: bias | marks shift because the factors of evaluation are imposed; the audience and the questions matter |

**Six questions before launching:** кого опросить; что исследовать; как провести опрос; как измерять
удовлетворённость; как трактовать значения; как использовать опрос для высокого уровня сервиса. Detailed questions
start from immersion into the client's world: which factors matter to him and by which he tells companies apart —
the **attributes**: продукт, скорость, сотрудники, компания, цена.

**Working with the CSI result:**
1. Look at the dynamics; focus on the area with the biggest failure (effort goes to the failure, not spread evenly).
2. Do not try to reach 100% — set real goals.
3. Find the cause of the drop: in-depth interviews and observation.
4. Identify the obstacles (employees not knowing the standards, technical limits).
5. Make an improvement plan with dates.
6. Evaluate the changes in a new survey.

CSI measures satisfaction, not loyalty and not competitiveness.

## 5. CSAT

**CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Survey)** — satisfaction after a specific interaction with a product or channel at
critical points of the path (after a support request, after a payment). CSAT = the average mark on a five-point scale,
to hundredths. Пример для расчёта (не из курса): 5, 4, 4, 3, 2, 2 → 20 / 6 = 3,33. It is the arithmetic mean, not the most frequent mark.

**Do not mix:** NPS — willingness to recommend; CSI — satisfaction across many factors; CSAT — satisfaction after a
specific interaction.

## 6. Seven stages of analysing quantitative data

1. Combine the data from all sources and touchpoints.
2. Analyse the values, metrics, shares, percentages, significant indicators.
3. Compare by categories (businesses, products, significant deviations).
4. Compare with trends / find trends (dynamics).
5. Compare with benchmarks by regions, products, industry.
6. Determine the significance of the factors, find the drivers of loyalty.
7. Visualise the results (a service dashboard).

## 7. From a metric back to the client

A metric is a signal, not the problem. Go from the drop to the scenario: which touchpoint, which client, what happens
to him — then a problem statement with a person and a consequence and a «Как мы можем…» question on the client's
problem, not «как повысить NPS» (`analysis.md` §7). A metric that is declared but not shown failing in the CJM is a
weak link between pain and metric.

## 8. Index questionnaires (when the user needs the index itself)

- **NPS:** two questions — «Какова вероятность Вашей рекомендации компании другу или коллеге?» on the scale, and a
  request to explain the main reason for the mark.
- **CSI:** first the six questions before launch (§4) and the attributes that matter to this client; a short form
  keeps one main question and 3–4 attribute questions (скорость and other parameters) — completion stays good and
  clients still leave a comment. CSI is asked of active users of the product.
- **CSAT:** one question on the five-point scale right after a specific interaction at a critical point (after a
  support request, after a payment).
- Channels for index surveys: mostly SMS, e-mail, robotic calls; a widget in digital channels.
