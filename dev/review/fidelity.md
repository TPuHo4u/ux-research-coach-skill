# Review round 1 — fidelity lens (2026-10-22, fresh agent, read-only)

I reviewed SKILL.md and the four reference files against the module 1 and 2 slide texts, the workbook, the slides of the «Количественные исследования» and «Поиск решений» webinars, the summary of the quantitative-research webinar (made from an automatic transcript) and the cheat-*.html fragments. I modified no files. Every quote was checked to appear verbatim exactly once in the skill files.

There are 30 findings: 1 critical, 10 important and 19 minor. Most definitions and numbers are faithful to the course: the double diamond, the five stages, «Впечатление = Факт − Ожидание», «Система + Человек», the CJM periods and contents, the Service Blueprint layers and steps, the five key errors, the steps of Фокусировка, the project task wording, the NPS groups and formula, CSI facts and its six steps, the CSAT formula, the 5% and ~20% sample thresholds, the three survey blocks, the brainstorm rules, the five hats and the feedback sheet. Most outside additions are correctly marked «вывод, не из курса» in the references.

The real problems are in four places.

1. **Step order (critical).** The routing order 1→2→3→4→5→6 puts the survey after Фокусировка. The course checks problem hypotheses quantitatively before choosing the key problem: module 2 slide 27, and the webinar summary [00:03:51].

2. **Survey mode (important).**
   - Its fact-only rewrite rule leaves no way to check how significant a problem is for the client, which is one of the course's three tasks for quantitative research.
   - It allows an NPS question on request, without the «вывод, не из курса» mark, although the lecturer said recommendation is not measured in this survey.
   - «share of the target audience» leaves the base of the percentage unclear; the course reports «процент респондентов».

3. **Contradictions between SKILL.md and the references (important).**
   - SKILL.md says an insight is a hypothesis; analysis.md and the course say it is not.
   - The blanket ban on improvement plans contradicts mode 7's course-backed plan with dates.
   - The guide-wording rules flag «обычно». The course itself says to ask the client to show how he usually goes through the scenario, and its own guide examples would fail the skill's rules.

4. **Narrowed or unmarked course rules.**
   - Important: research needs at least two methods, the second used at least three times; per-channel lanes in a CJM recreate the «размытый сценарий» error; at the investment committee a problem is confirmed by insights plus a quantitative check, not insights alone.
   - Minor: wording, markers, pointers, and examples presented without a mark.

Three limits to keep in mind:
- The summary of the quantitative-research webinar comes from an automatic transcript. Oral points, including the Sberopros guest's remarks on scales, are marked as oral in the evidence.
- The «инсайт — не гипотеза» line comes from the cheat fragment, likely built from exam questions, not from a slide.
- I did not read the module 3 materials or the full webinar transcripts.
